## 🤖 Featured Project – VoltCare AI

**VoltCare AI – Electric Vehicle Customer & Service Agent**

### 🚩 Problem Statement

Electric vehicle owners may struggle to find information about available services, understand maintenance costs and schedule service appointments. Service teams also spend time handling repetitive enquiries, collecting customer details and managing appointment requests manually.

### 💡 Proposed Solution

VoltCare AI is an intelligent, tool-using AI agent designed to simplify the electric vehicle service process. It acts as a virtual service representative, understanding customer requests, checking company service information and performing service-related actions through connected tools.

### ⚙️ Key Features

- **Customer Assistance:** Understands customer requests and answers common electric vehicle service-related questions.
- **Service Verification:** Checks whether the company provides the requested service and clearly informs customers when a service is unavailable.
- **Price Estimation:** Retrieves estimated prices for supported services.
- **Customer Information Collection:** Collects customer details, vehicle model, service requirements and preferred appointment dates.
- **Appointment Scheduling:** Checks available appointment slots and books appointments when a suitable slot is available.
- **Data Validation:** Uses Pydantic to validate customer details and appointment information before processing.
- **Enquiry and Booking Management:** Stores service enquiries and confirmed appointment details in Google Sheets.
- **Tool-Based Actions:** Uses AI agent tools to check services, retrieve prices, verify appointment availability and process bookings.

### 🎯 Project Goal

To build an intelligent AI service agent that goes beyond answering questions by making decisions, using tools and performing real service-related actions. VoltCare AI aims to simplify the customer service journey, reduce repetitive manual work and make EV service interactions more efficient.

*Note: The agent should only confirm appointments after successful booking through an actual scheduling system. If a service or appointment slot is unavailable, it will clearly inform the customer instead of making false confirmations.*
